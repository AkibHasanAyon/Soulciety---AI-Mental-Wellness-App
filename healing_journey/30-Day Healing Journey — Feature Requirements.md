## 1. Feature Overview

### 1.1 Summary

The 30-Day Healing Journey is a new core feature of Soulciety. It provides each user with a fully AI-personalized, 30-day guided healing program — generated once during onboarding based on their answers to 10 assessment questions, then delivered day-by-day as structured daily sessions.

The journey is not a generic program. The AI analyzes the user's responses to identify their core trauma type, emotional state, dominant life wound, and primary healing goal — then generates 30 sequential themes that are logically ordered to move the user from pain, to awareness, to identity shift, to embodied healing.

### 1.2 Client Vision

- A 30-day guided journey for users to heal, align, and elevate consciousness.
- Daily sessions should be quick and doable — approximately 10–15 minutes.
- Tone must be supportive, uplifting, and higher-consciousness focused — not religious.
- Users should feel tangible growth, increased self-awareness, and deeper connection to themselves.

### 1.3 Chosen Approach: AI-Generated & Personalized Content

The client selected **Option 1: AI-generated personalized content**.

- Each user receives a unique healing plan generated by Gemini AI.
- Content is text-based with optional text-to-speech audio playback.
- The client does not control individual content — the AI generates it based on the user's assessment.
- **Trade-off acknowledged:** The client cannot manually edit or override AI-generated daily content.

### 1.4 Philosophical Alignment

All AI-generated content must align with Soulciety's three internalized philosophical pillars (never mentioned by name):
- **Identity (Neville Goddard):** "I AM" declarations, living in the end, revision.
- **Neuroscience (Joe Dispenza):** Breaking patterns, elevated emotions, identity rewiring.
- **Vibration (Abraham Hicks):** Emotions as GPS, reaching for better-feeling thoughts.

## 2. Onboarding & Assessment Flow

### 2.1 Overview

When a user starts the 30-Day Healing Journey for the first time, they complete a 10-question onboarding assessment. This is a **one-time flow** — it generates their personalized 30-day plan and is not repeated unless the user explicitly resets their journey (see Section 8.5).

The questions are **not personalized** — they are the same for every user. The AI personalizes the output (themes and content) based on the answers.

### 2.2 The 10 Assessment Questions

Each question is tagged with what it detects — this metadata is used during AI plan generation.

---

**Q1. Emotional Baseline**  
Type: `single_select` (MCQ)  
Prompt: *"How have you been feeling most of the time lately? Choose the one that resonates most."*

Options:
- Numb or disconnected
- Anxious or on edge
- Sad or emotionally heavy
- Angry or restless
- Lost or without direction
- Surprisingly okay

Detects: Current emotional state / depression / anxiety baseline

---

**Q2. Past Influence**  
Type: `scale` (1–5)  
Prompt: *"How much does your past affect how you feel about yourself today?"*

Scale: 1 = Not at all → 5 = Completely

Detects: Unresolved past trauma weight / degree of past anchoring

---

**Q3. Life Area Impact**  
Type: `single_select` (MCQ)  
Prompt: *"Which area of your life feels most affected right now?"*

Options:
- Relationships & connection
- Self-worth & identity
- Career & purpose
- Body & physical health
- Family & belonging
- Inner peace & mental calm

Detects: Domain of pain / where healing is most needed

---

**Q4. Core Pain Source**  
Type: `single_select` (MCQ)  
Prompt: *"What kind of experiences have hurt you the most?"*

Options:
- Feeling ignored, unseen, or emotionally neglected
- Being rejected or abandoned
- Being criticized, judged, or not good enough
- Feeling controlled, pressured, or powerless
- I don't fully know — just that something feels off

Detects: Core trauma type (critical for theme ordering)

---

**Q5. Coping Pattern**  
Type: `single_select` (MCQ)  
Prompt: *"When emotional pain gets intense, what do you usually do?"*

Options:
- Withdraw from people
- Overwork or stay constantly busy
- Distract myself (phone, content, etc.)
- Overeat or lose appetite
- Seek comfort from someone
- Sit with it quietly

Detects: Avoidance vs. regulation vs. dependency coping style

---

**Q6. Relationship Fear**  
Type: `single_select` (MCQ)  
Prompt: *"In close relationships, what do you fear most?"*

Options:

- Being controlled or losing independence
- Not being valued or chosen
- Getting too close to someone

Detects: Attachment style / relational trauma pattern

**Q7. Core Belief**

- "I don't know who I am"

Detects: Identity wound / subconscious limiting belief

Detects: Blockers / readiness level / resistance pattern

### 2.3 Onboarding UX Flow

```
App → Healing Journey Tab
  → "Start Your Healing Journey" CTA
      (only shown if no active HealingAssessment exists for this user)
  → Introduction screen (brief, 2–3 sentences about what's ahead)
  → Question 1 of 10
  → ... (progress bar shown)
  → Question 10 of 10
  → POST /api/healing/assessment/
  → Loading screen: "Your personalized healing journey is being created..."
      (client polls GET /api/healing/assessment/status/ every 3 seconds)
  → On status = 'ready':
      → Success screen: "Your 30-Day Journey Begins Today"
      → Day 1 unlocked immediately
  → On status = 'failed':
      → Error screen: "Something went wrong. Please try again."
      → CTA triggers re-submission (existing assessment record is reset)
```

### 2.4 Re-submission Guard

The `POST /api/healing/assessment/` endpoint checks for an existing `HealingAssessment` for the requesting user:
- If `status = 'ready'` → return `HTTP 409 Conflict` with `"message": "Your journey is already active."`
- If `status = 'failed'` → allow re-submission: overwrite answers, reset status to `'pending'`, re-trigger Celery task.
- If `status = 'generating'` **or `status = 'pending'`** → return `HTTP 202` and instruct client to keep polling. **Do not create a second task.** This covers the double-tap race condition where the first `transaction.on_commit()` task has been queued but not yet picked up by the worker, leaving status as `'pending'`.

### 2.5 Assessment Storage

All 10 answers are stored in a `HealingAssessment` model (see Section 7). The raw answers + AI-generated analysis are both persisted.

## 3. AI Healing Plan Generation

### 3.1 Overview

After the user submits their 10-question assessment, a Celery task calls Gemini AI to:

1. **Analyze** the answers and produce a structured healing profile.
2. **Generate** 30 day-by-day themes — ordered to create a meaningful arc from pain → awareness → shift → embodiment.
3. **Store** the 30 themes in the database, ready to be unlocked day by day.
4. **Initialize** all supporting records: `HealingDay` rows, `HealingStreak`, `HealingJourneySettings`.

> **Important:** Daily content (reflection, affirmation, exercises, etc.) is NOT generated at plan creation time. It is generated fresh on the morning of each day (see Section 9.2). Only the **themes** are generated upfront. The sole exception is Day 1, whose content is generated immediately as part of plan generation so it is ready the moment the user taps in.

### 3.2 Healing Profile Analysis

The AI produces a structured healing profile JSON from the assessment answers:

```json
{
  "emotional_state": "anxious and disconnected",
  "trauma_type": "abandonment and emotional neglect",
  "core_wound": "I am not enough",
  "life_domain": "relationships and self-worth",
  "coping_style": "withdrawal and distraction",
  "attachment_pattern": "fear of abandonment",
  "primary_healing_goal": "relief from self-doubt and loneliness",
  "healing_blockers": "fear of uncovering pain",
  "personal_desire": "[user's exact words from Q10]",
  "healing_arc_summary": "A short 2–3 sentence description of this user's healing arc"
}
```

This profile is stored in the `HealingAssessment` model and injected into all future AI prompts for this user's journey.

### 3.3 30-Day Theme Generation

The AI generates 30 themes in a logical, therapeutic arc. The arc must follow this general structure:

| Phase | Days | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | 1–5 | Safety, grounding, acknowledging pain |
| Awareness | 6–10 | Identifying patterns, understanding wounds |
| Release | 11–15 | Letting go, forgiveness, grief processing |
| Rewiring | 16–20 | New beliefs, identity work, self-compassion |
| Embodiment | 21–25 | Living the new self, relationships, abundance |
| Integration | 26–30 | Wholeness, vision, closing the loop |

Each theme is stored as:

```json
{
  "day_number": 1,
  "theme_title": "Coming Home to Yourself",
  "theme_intention": "Today you give yourself permission to begin.",
  "theme_category": "Foundation",
  "healing_focus": ["self-compassion", "grounding", "safety"]
}
```

### 3.4 System Prompt for Plan Generation (Developer Reference)

The Gemini call must include:
- All 10 assessment answers (labeled)
- Instruction to produce a `healing_profile` JSON block
- Instruction to produce a `themes` array of 30 objects
- Instruction that themes must follow the 6-phase arc
- Instruction that themes must be specifically tailored to the user's trauma type, life domain, and healing goal — not generic
- Instruction to return only valid JSON (no markdown fences, no preamble)

Model: `gemini-3.1-pro-preview`  
Max tokens: 4000

## 4. Daily Journey Structure

### 4.1 Timezone Handling

The user's IANA timezone string (e.g. `"America/New_York"`) is captured during onboarding and stored on `HealingJourneySettings`. All day-unlock logic and reminder scheduling uses this timezone.

If the user's timezone is not known (e.g. not provided by the mobile client), default to `UTC`.

### 4.2 Day Unlock Logic

- **Day 1** is unlocked immediately when `HealingAssessment.status` transitions to `'ready'`. Its `unlock_date` is set to `today` in the user's local timezone.
- Each subsequent day's `unlock_date` is set to `journey_started_date + (day_number - 1) days` at plan generation time — all 30 dates are pre-calculated and stored.
- A Celery Beat task runs **hourly** to transition `HealingDay` records from `locked` → `available` for any user whose local time has crossed midnight (see Section 9.4).
- A user **cannot skip ahead** to future (`locked`) days.
- A user **can always revisit past days** in read-only mode (they cannot re-submit mood or re-mark complete).

### 4.3 Day Completion Logic

A day is marked **complete** when the user taps "Complete Today's Session."

> **Streak preservation:** A user can mark a day complete even if they haven't consumed every module. Completion = they engaged with the day. This prevents streaks from breaking due to life circumstances.

### 4.4 Day States

| State | Description |
|---|---|
| `locked` | Future day — not yet accessible |
| `available` | Day is unlocked and interactive (today or a past day not yet completed/missed) |
| `completed` | Day marked done by user |
| `missed` | Day's window closed without completion (streak breaks, unless grace period applies) |

### 4.5 Grace Period for Missed Days

A user who completes a `missed` day before the **end of the following local day** does NOT permanently lose their streak. The grace window is defined as "until 23:59 of the day after the miss" in the user's own timezone — a predictable contract the UI can display as "You have until tonight to make it up."

Implementation:
- When a day transitions to `missed`, `HealingStreak.current_streak` is NOT zeroed immediately.
- Instead, `HealingStreak.grace_period_active` is set to `True` and `HealingStreak.grace_expires_at` is set to **23:59:59 of the following local day** for that user, calculated using their `HealingJourneySettings.timezone`. Example: a user in `America/New_York` who misses on Monday gets `grace_expires_at = Tuesday 23:59:59 ET` (stored as UTC).
  - This avoids the floating-window problem of `now + 24h`, where the expiry time shifts based on task queue delays.
- The `check_missed_days_and_streaks` task runs hourly. If `grace_period_active` is True and `grace_expires_at > now (UTC)`: leave streak intact, do nothing.
- If the user completes the missed day before `grace_expires_at`: the day transitions to `completed`, `grace_period_active` resets to `False`, `grace_expires_at` is cleared, and the streak continues unbroken.
- If the user's local time crosses `grace_expires_at` without completion, the next hourly run of `check_missed_days_and_streaks` zeroes `current_streak` and clears both grace fields.

## 5. Daily Content Modules

Each day's session is composed of 7 modules. All content is generated by Gemini AI on the morning of each day (see Section 9.2), tailored to that day's theme AND the user's healing profile.

If content generation has not yet completed when the user opens a day, a loading state is shown and the client polls `GET /api/healing/days/<day_number>/` every 5 seconds. If content generation has **failed** (after 3 retries), fallback content is served (see Section 10.3).

### 5.1 Module 1: Guided Reflection

**What it is:** A short, poetic, healing text passage explaining the day's theme and how it connects to the user's healing journey. Written in second person ("you"), warm, and grounded.

**Length:** 200–350 words  
**Tone:** Like a wise, gentle mentor speaking directly to the user  
**TTS:** Yes — user can play this as audio via text-to-speech  
**Format stored:** Plain text (with paragraph breaks)

**Example (theme "Coming Home to Yourself"):**
> *"There's a part of you that has been waiting to be welcomed back. Not the version you think you should be — the raw, unedited, honest version of you who got tired of performing..."*

---

### 5.2 Module 2: Daily Affirmation

**What it is:** One powerful, present-tense identity affirmation aligned with the day's theme.

**Length:** 1–3 sentences maximum  
**Tone:** Declarative, identity-rooted, not toxic positivity ("I AM" language preferred)  
**TTS:** Yes — user can play this as audio  
**Format stored:** Plain text

**Example:**  
*"I am allowed to exist exactly as I am. My healing does not have to be earned. I am already worthy of the peace I seek."*

---

### 5.3 Module 3: Mental Workout

**What it is:** A short cognitive or mindset exercise. Not meditation — an active mental practice.

**Length:** 100–200 words (instructions) + the exercise itself  
**Examples:**
- "Write down 3 thoughts you had about yourself today. Now rewrite each one as if your best friend were speaking to you."
- "Identify one belief that kept you small today. Finish this sentence: 'I used to believe ___ but now I choose to see ___."
- "Think of a memory that hurt. Without reliving it, ask: what did that moment teach me about what I need?"

**TTS:** No  
**Format stored:** Plain text

---

### 5.4 Module 4: Physical Workout

**What it is:** A brief, accessible, body-based practice (no equipment required). Focused on somatic release, not fitness.

**Length:** 100–200 words (instructions)  
**Examples:**
- 5-minute slow walk with intentional breathing
- Gentle neck and shoulder stretches for releasing tension
- Shaking exercise (2 minutes of shaking limbs to release stored stress)
- Progressive muscle relaxation (squeeze and release muscle groups)

**Note:** These should never be intense fitness workouts. Always gentle, trauma-informed, body-aware.  
**TTS:** No  
**Format stored:** Plain text

---

### 5.5 Module 5: Breathing Exercise

**What it is:** A structured breathwork practice with specific counts. Always short — 3–5 minutes.

**Format stored:** Structured JSON:
```json
{
  "name": "Box Breathing",
  "description": "This practice brings your nervous system into balance...",
  "inhale_seconds": 4,
  "hold_in_seconds": 4,
  "exhale_seconds": 4,
  "hold_out_seconds": 4,
  "rounds": 5
}
```

**Validation rule:** `hold_in_seconds` must be ≤ 8 and `exhale_seconds` must be ≤ 10. The AI prompt enforces this; backend also validates before saving.

**UI Note:** The mobile app renders an animated breathing guide (expand/contract circle) using these values. The AI provides the values; the animation is built in the frontend.

---

### 5.6 Module 6: Mini Challenge

**What it is:** A small, real-life action the user takes out in the world today. Not a reflection — a behavior.

**Length:** 1–2 sentences  
**Examples:**
- "Send a text to one person today saying something you genuinely appreciate about them."
- "Tonight, before you sleep, place your hand on your chest and say out loud: 'I am safe. I am here.'"
- "The next time you catch yourself in negative self-talk, pause. Take one breath. Then say: 'I am learning.'"

**TTS:** No  
**Format stored:** Plain text

---

### 5.7 Module 7: Journal Prompt + Mood Check-In

#### 7a. Journal Prompt

A single, deep, open-ended question tied to the day's theme. Instructs the user to write in the Soulciety Journal feature.

**Format:** One sentence question  
**Example:** *"What is one thing you've been carrying that no longer belongs to you — and what would it feel like to set it down?"*

**Integration:** Tapping "Write in Journal" opens the Soulciety Journal (existing `journal` app) with this prompt pre-filled as the entry prompt/tag. The journal entry's `healing_day` FK is set upon creation so the entry links back to this healing day.

#### 7b. Mood Check-In

At the end of the session, the user selects their current mood.

**Options** (matching existing `MoodEntry` model):
- 😊 Happy
- 😨 Fear
- 😔 Sad
- 😠 Angry
- 🤢 Sick

**Storage:** Creates a `MoodEntry` via the existing mood system (`app_home` app). `HealingDay.mood_entry` FK is updated to point to this entry. Mood selection is optional — a day can be marked complete without logging a mood.

## 6. Progress & Streak System

### 6.1 Streak Tracking

- Streak = consecutive days completed without a missed day (outside the grace period).
- `HealingStreak.current_streak` increments by 1 on each day completion.
- `HealingStreak.longest_streak` updates when `current_streak` exceeds it.
- `HealingStreak.total_days_completed` increments on each completion (never decrements).
- `HealingStreak` is created (with all zeroes) as part of the `generate_healing_plan` task — it is never absent for a user with an active plan.

### 6.2 Grace Period (see Section 4.5 for full logic)

- `grace_period_active` and `grace_expires_at` on `HealingStreak` control the 24-hour grace window.
- Streak is only zeroed after grace expires without completion.

### 6.3 Milestone Rewards

Milestone notifications are sent at day completions: 3, 7, 14, 21, and 30.

| Day | Message |
|---|---|
| 3 | "3-day streak! You're building something real." |
| 7 | "One full week. Your nervous system is changing." |
| 14 | "Halfway there. You should be proud." |
| 21 | "21 days. This is who you are now." |
| 30 | "You did it. 30 days of choosing yourself." |

Milestones are delivered as **in-app notifications + push notifications**. `HealingStreak.milestones_reached` stores the list of reached milestone day numbers (e.g. `[3, 7]`) so they are never re-sent.

### 6.4 Progress Visualization

- Progress bar: `X / 30 days completed`
- Day grid: 30 day tiles showing state (`locked` / `available` / `completed` / `missed`)
- Current streak counter with flame icon
- Milestone badges (visual unlocks at 3, 7, 14, 21, 30)

### 6.5 Journey Completion

On Day 30 completion:
- Full completion screen: "You've completed your 30-Day Healing Journey."
- Summary shown: healing arc summary + days completed + longest streak.
- `HealingAssessment.journey_completed_at` is set to `now`.
- Option A: **Start a new journey** — triggers the reset flow (see Section 8.5).
- Option B: **Archive this journey** — journey is read-only; user can browse all 30 days as history.

### 7.1 HealingAssessment

Stores the user's onboarding answers, AI-generated healing profile, and journey lifecycle timestamps.

```python
class HealingAssessment(models.Model):
    user = models.OneToOneField(
        settings.AUTH_USER_MODEL,
        on_delete=models.CASCADE,
        related_name='healing_assessment'
    )
    created_at = models.DateTimeField(auto_now_add=True)
    updated_at = models.DateTimeField(auto_now=True)

    # Journey lifecycle timestamps
    journey_started_at = models.DateTimeField(null=True, blank=True)
    # Set when status transitions to 'ready'. Used to calculate all unlock dates.

    journey_completed_at = models.DateTimeField(null=True, blank=True)
    # Set when Day 30 is marked complete.

    # Raw answers from the 10 questions
    # All MCQ fields use max_length=255 to safely accommodate any future copy changes
    # on the frontend without risking DB truncation errors.
    q1_emotional_baseline = models.CharField(max_length=255)
    q2_past_influence     = models.IntegerField()                 # Scale 1–5
    q3_life_area_impact   = models.CharField(max_length=255)
    q4_core_pain_source   = models.CharField(max_length=255)
    q5_coping_pattern     = models.CharField(max_length=255)
    q6_relationship_fear  = models.CharField(max_length=255)
    q7_core_belief        = models.CharField(max_length=255)
    q8_desired_relief     = models.CharField(max_length=255)
    q9_healing_resistance = models.CharField(max_length=255)
    q10_personal_desire   = models.TextField(max_length=500)

    # AI-generated healing profile (JSON — see Section 3.2)
    healing_profile = models.JSONField(null=True, blank=True)

    # Plan generation status
    STATUS_CHOICES = [
        ('pending',    'Pending'),
        ('generating', 'Generating'),
        ('ready',      'Ready'),
        ('failed',     'Failed'),
    ]
    status           = models.CharField(max_length=20, choices=STATUS_CHOICES, default='pending')
    generation_error = models.TextField(blank=True)

    @property
    def journey_start_date(self):
        """Returns the DATE portion of journey_started_at in UTC. Used for unlock date math."""
        if self.journey_started_at:
            return self.journey_started_at.date()
        return None

    def __str__(self):
        return f"HealingAssessment for {self.user} ({self.status})"
```

    content_status = models.CharField(max_length=20, choices=CONTENT_STATUS_CHOICES, default='pending')

## 9. Celery Background Tasks

All tasks reside in `healing_journey/tasks.py`.

---

### 9.1 Task: `generate_healing_plan`

**Trigger:** Called via `transaction.on_commit()` immediately after `HealingAssessment` is saved with `status='pending'`.  
**Queue:** Default Celery worker  
**Retries:** 3, with 60-second backoff  
**Max tokens:** 4000

**Steps:**
1. Set `HealingAssessment.status = 'generating'`. Save.
2. Build Gemini prompt from all 10 assessment answers (see Section 10.2).
3. Call `gemini-3.1-pro-preview` with plan generation system prompt.
4. Parse JSON response → extract `healing_profile` + `themes` array (30 items).
5. Validate: confirm exactly 30 themes with sequential `day_number` 1–30.
6. Save `healing_profile` to `HealingAssessment`.
7. Set `HealingAssessment.journey_started_at = now()`.
8. Atomically create:
   - 30 `HealingTheme` records.
   - 30 `HealingDay` records with pre-calculated `unlock_date` values (`journey_start_date + N - 1` days). Day 1: `state='available'`. Days 2–30: `state='locked'`.
   - `HealingStreak` (all zeroes, `grace_period_active=False`).
   - `HealingJourneySettings` (with timezone from assessment submission header, or `'UTC'`).
9. Set `HealingAssessment.status = 'ready'`. Save.
10. Trigger `generate_day_content` task for Day 1 immediately (so content is ready before the user taps in).
11. Send push notification: "🌿 Your 30-Day Healing Journey is ready. Day 1 awaits."
12. Send in-app notification (`healing_plan_ready`).

**On failure (after all retries):**
- Set `HealingAssessment.status = 'failed'`, store error in `generation_error`.
- Send in-app notification: "Something went wrong creating your journey. Please try again."

---

### 9.2 Task: `generate_day_content`

Generates all 7 content modules for a single `HealingDay`.

**Triggers:**
- Directly after `generate_healing_plan` for Day 1 (immediate).
- From `generate_day_content_batch` (Celery Beat, 6 AM UTC — pre-generates tomorrow's content for all users).
- On-demand from `GET /api/healing/days/<day_number>/` if `content_status='pending'` and the day is `available` (prevents a user from ever seeing a spinner with no recovery path).

**Steps:**
1. Set `HealingDay.content_status = 'generating'`. Save.
2. Build content generation prompt using `HealingTheme` + `healing_profile` (see Section 10.3).
3. Call `gemini-3.1-pro-preview`.
4. Parse and validate JSON response (check all 7 fields present; validate breathing exercise counts).
5. Create `DayContent` record with `is_fallback=False`.
6. Set `HealingDay.content_status = 'ready'`. Save.

**On failure (after 3 retries):**
- Set `HealingDay.content_status = 'failed'`.
- Trigger `serve_fallback_content` task (see Section 9.7).

**Retries:** 3, with 30-second backoff  
**Max tokens:** 4500

---

### 9.3 Task: `generate_day_content_batch`

**Trigger:** Celery Beat — `0 6 * * *` (daily at 6 AM UTC).  
**Beat schedule name:** `pre-generate-healing-content`

**Steps:**
1. Find all `HealingDay` records where `unlock_date = tomorrow` AND `content_status = 'pending'` AND `state = 'locked'` (i.e., will unlock tomorrow).
2. For each, dispatch `generate_day_content` as a separate Celery task.

> **Why "tomorrow"?** Content is generated the night before so it's ready the moment the day unlocks at midnight. Today's content edge case (user submits assessment late at night) is handled by the on-demand trigger in `generate_day_content`.

---

### 9.4 Task: `unlock_healing_days`

**Trigger:** Celery Beat — `0 * * * *` (every hour, on the hour).  
**Beat schedule name:** `unlock-healing-days`

**Why hourly?** Users are spread across all timezones. A single daily UTC run would unlock days at wildly different local times — some users would see tomorrow's day unlock at 2 PM. Running hourly ensures each user's day unlocks within ~1 hour of their local midnight.

**Steps:**
1. Fetch all `HealingDay` records where `state = 'locked'`.
2. For each record, look up the user's `HealingJourneySettings.timezone`.
3. Convert `now (UTC)` to the user's local time using `pytz` or `zoneinfo`.
4. If the user's **local date** is `>=` the day's `unlock_date`, transition `state = 'locked' → 'available'`. Save.
5. Log unlock count per run.

```python
# Pseudocode for the per-user check inside the task
from zoneinfo import ZoneInfo
from django.utils import timezone

now_utc = timezone.now()
user_tz = ZoneInfo(settings_obj.timezone)  # from HealingJourneySettings
local_now = now_utc.astimezone(user_tz)
local_today = local_now.date()

if healing_day.unlock_date <= local_today and healing_day.state == 'locked':
    healing_day.state = 'available'
    healing_day.save(update_fields=['state'])
```

---

### 9.5 Task: `check_missed_days_and_streaks`

**Trigger:** Celery Beat — `0 * * * *` (every hour, on the hour).  
**Beat schedule name:** `check-healing-streaks`

**Why hourly?** A fixed `23:59 UTC` run would mark East Coast users' days as missed at 7:59 PM local time — while they still have over 4 hours left to complete the session. Running hourly means a day is only marked missed once the user's **own local time** crosses 23:59.

**Steps:**
1. Fetch all `HealingDay` records where `state = 'available'` and `completed_at IS NULL`.
2. For each, look up `HealingJourneySettings.timezone` for the user.
3. Convert `now (UTC)` to the user's local time.
4. If the user's **local date** is **strictly greater than** `unlock_date` (i.e., the day has fully passed in their timezone):
   a. Set `HealingDay.state = 'missed'`. Save.
   b. Retrieve the user's `HealingStreak`.
   c. **Grace period logic:**
      - If `grace_period_active = False`:
        - Set `grace_period_active = True`.
        - Set `grace_expires_at` = **23:59:59 of tomorrow in the user's local timezone**, converted to UTC. This gives the user until the end of the next full local day to recover — a predictable, communicable contract.
        - Do NOT zero `current_streak` yet. Save.
      - If `grace_period_active = True` AND `now_utc < grace_expires_at`:
        - Grace window still open — do nothing. Streak preserved.
      - If `grace_period_active = True` AND `now_utc >= grace_expires_at`:
        - Grace window expired. Set `current_streak = 0`, `grace_period_active = False`, `grace_expires_at = None`. Save.

```python
# Pseudocode: calculating grace_expires_at
from zoneinfo import ZoneInfo
from datetime import datetime, time, timedelta

user_tz = ZoneInfo(settings_obj.timezone)
local_now = now_utc.astimezone(user_tz)
tomorrow_local = (local_now + timedelta(days=1)).date()
grace_local_dt = datetime.combine(tomorrow_local, time(23, 59, 59), tzinfo=user_tz)
grace_expires_at_utc = grace_local_dt.astimezone(ZoneInfo('UTC'))
```

---

### 9.6 Task: `send_morning_healing_reminder`

**Trigger:** Celery Beat — `0 * * * *` (every hour, on the hour).  
**Beat schedule name:** `morning-healing-reminder`

**Why hourly?** A fixed `07:00 UTC` run sends "morning" notifications at 1 PM in Dhaka and 2–3 AM on the U.S. East Coast. Running hourly and checking local time ensures each user receives their reminder at their own configured time (default 07:00 local).

**Steps:**
1. Find all users with an active `HealingAssessment` (`status='ready'`, `journey_completed_at=None`).
2. Filter to users where `HealingJourneySettings.morning_reminder_enabled = True` AND `UserProfile.notifications_enabled = True`.
3. For each user:
   a. Get their `HealingJourneySettings.timezone` and `morning_reminder_time`.
   b. Convert `now (UTC)` to their local time.
   c. If the user's **local hour matches** `morning_reminder_time.hour` (within the current hour window):
      - Check if today's `HealingDay` is `available` and NOT `completed`.
      - If yes: send push notification — `"🌿 Day [X] is waiting for you. Your healing continues today."`

---

### 9.7 Task: `serve_fallback_content`

**Trigger:** Called when `generate_day_content` exhausts all retries.

**Steps:**
1. Select fallback content from `FALLBACK_CONTENT_BANK` in `healing_journey/fallbacks.py` — a hardcoded list of 6 generic but valid day content sets (one per healing phase).
2. Choose the set matching the day's `theme_category` (Foundation / Awareness / Release / Rewiring / Embodiment / Integration).
3. Create `DayContent` record with `is_fallback = True`.
4. Set `HealingDay.content_status = 'ready'`. Save.
5. Log the fallback event (for monitoring/alerting).

> **Fallback bank** (`healing_journey/fallbacks.py`) must be implemented as part of this feature. Minimum 6 content sets (one per phase). These are the safety net — users never see a broken empty screen.

### 9.8 Task: `send_evening_healing_reminder`

**Trigger:** Celery Beat — `0 * * * *` (every hour, on the hour — **same Beat entry as morning reminder**).  
**Beat schedule name:** `evening-healing-reminder`

> **Implementation note:** Both morning and evening reminder checks can be combined into a single hourly task (`send_healing_reminders`) that dispatches the correct notification type based on which configured reminder hour the current UTC time maps to for each user. This avoids running two separate hourly tasks over the same user set. The Beat schedule entry fires once per hour; the task logic determines which users receive morning vs. evening notifications based on their local time.

**Steps:**
1. Find all users with active journeys where today's day is `available` and NOT `completed`.
2. Filter to users where `HealingJourneySettings.evening_reminder_enabled = True` AND `UserProfile.notifications_enabled = True`.
3. For each user:
   a. Get their `HealingJourneySettings.timezone` and `evening_reminder_time`.
   b. Convert `now (UTC)` to their local time.
   c. If the user's **local hour matches** `evening_reminder_time.hour`:
      - Send push notification: `"🌙 Before the day ends — 10 minutes for yourself. You're worth it."`

### 9.9 Task: `send_milestone_notification`

**Trigger:** Inline, called synchronously inside the `POST /api/healing/days/<day_number>/complete/` view after streak update.

**Steps:**
1. Check if the newly completed `day_number` is in `[3, 7, 14, 21, 30]`.
2. Check that it's NOT already in `HealingStreak.milestones_reached` (idempotency guard).
3. If both conditions met: send in-app + push notification with the milestone message.
4. Append `day_number` to `HealingStreak.milestones_reached`. Save.

### 10.4 JSON Validation

After receiving each Gemini response, validate before saving:

**Plan generation:**
- `healing_profile` has all 10 required keys.
- `themes` is an array of exactly 30 objects.
- Each theme has `day_number` (1–30, no duplicates), `theme_title`, `theme_intention`, `theme_category` (one of the 6 valid phases), `healing_focus` (non-empty list).

**Daily content generation:**
- All 7 top-level keys present and non-empty.
- `breathing_exercise` has all 6 required keys.
- `breathing_exercise.inhale_seconds` between 3–6.
- `breathing_exercise.hold_in_seconds` between 0–8.
- `breathing_exercise.exhale_seconds` between 4–10.
- `breathing_exercise.rounds` between 3–8.

If validation fails, raise an exception to trigger Celery retry. After 3 retries, trigger fallback.

### 10.5 Fallback Content Bank

`healing_journey/fallbacks.py` must define `FALLBACK_CONTENT_BANK` — a dict keyed by `theme_category` with at least one complete content set per phase. These are hardcoded and reviewed by the team. They are used only when Gemini is unavailable after retries.

Healing reminders respect both layers:

2. Journey-specific preferences on `HealingJourneySettings` (`morning_reminder_enabled`, `evening_reminder_enabled`).

The morning and evening reminder times stored on `HealingJourneySettings` are used by the Celery Beat tasks to filter users whose local time matches the configured reminder hour (approximate, within the UTC batch window).

### 12.3 Celery Beat Schedule

Add to `CELERY_BEAT_SCHEDULE` in settings:

```python
# Runs hourly — unlocks days for users whose local time has crossed midnight
'unlock-healing-days': {
    'task': 'healing_journey.tasks.unlock_healing_days',
    'schedule': crontab(minute=0),          # Every hour, on the hour
},

# Runs once daily — pre-generates content for the following day
'pre-generate-healing-content': {
    'task': 'healing_journey.tasks.generate_day_content_batch',
    'schedule': crontab(hour=6, minute=0),  # 6 AM UTC daily
},

# Runs hourly — dispatches morning AND evening reminders based on each user's local time
'send-healing-reminders': {
    'task': 'healing_journey.tasks.send_healing_reminders',
    'schedule': crontab(minute=0),          # Every hour, on the hour
},

# Runs hourly — marks missed days and manages grace period based on each user's local time
'check-healing-streaks': {
    'task': 'healing_journey.tasks.check_missed_days_and_streaks',
    'schedule': crontab(minute=0),          # Every hour, on the hour
},
```

> **Note:** Three of the four tasks run on the same `crontab(minute=0)` schedule. They can be implemented as three separate tasks (clear separation of concerns) or merged into a single `hourly_healing_maintenance` task that calls each sub-routine in sequence. Either is acceptable; keep them separate for easier debugging and independent retry behavior.

### 12.4 Task Execution Pattern

```
Every hour (minute=0):
  → unlock_healing_days           — locks → available for users whose local midnight has passed
  → send_healing_reminders        — morning OR evening push, matched to each user's local hour
  → check_missed_days_and_streaks — missed states + grace period, matched to each user's local 23:59

06:00 UTC daily:
  → generate_day_content_batch    — pre-generate tomorrow's content for all active users

On-demand (triggered by Celery task chain):
  → generate_healing_plan         — on assessment submission
  → generate_day_content          — for Day 1 immediately; for other days if batch missed them
  → serve_fallback_content        — when generate_day_content exhausts retries
  → send_milestone_notification   — inline after day completion
```

> **Dependency note:** `pytz` or the stdlib `zoneinfo` (Python 3.9+) is required for timezone-aware local time calculations in the three hourly tasks. Django's `USE_TZ = True` must be confirmed set in settings.

### 12.7 One Journey Per User (Current Scope)

Each user can only have **one active (non-archived) healing journey** at a time. The `POST /api/healing/assessment/` guard (Section 2.4) enforces this.

When a user resets (Section 8.5), the old records are soft-archived (`is_archived=True`) and a fresh `HealingAssessment` is created. Future versions may add a journey history browser — out of scope for now.
